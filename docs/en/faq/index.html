FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Answers to common questions about design, materials, sampling, manufacturing and delivery.

Products & quantities

What products can HOUSE OF THREAD manufacture?

We provide custom sewing and manufacturing services across a wide range of categories, including men's, women's, and children's apparel, uniforms, workwear, hotel and institutional bedding, pillowcases, table linens, curtains, and other custom-sewn products. Each project is reviewed according to its technical requirements and production feasibility.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard minimum order starts at 50 pieces per design. Minimum quantities may vary depending on the product, materials, and production requirements. Special requests can be reviewed individually.

Can I order different sizes and colors within the same design?

Yes. Orders can include different sizes and colors, subject to the agreed production specifications and available materials. Size and color quantities are confirmed before production.

Do you manufacture products for independent brands and private labels?

Yes. We support independent brands and businesses that require products manufactured according to their own specifications. Labeling and packaging requirements can be discussed as part of the project.

Design, materials & samples

Do I need to have a finished design before contacting you?

Not at all. Whether you have a completed design, a reference image, or simply an idea, we can discuss your concept and help develop a suitable product for manufacturing.

Can you manufacture products using my own designs?

Yes. We work with client-provided designs, samples, and technical specifications. Our team reviews the requirements and discusses any adjustments needed before production.

Do you offer pattern making and size grading?

Pattern development and size grading can be included as part of the manufacturing process, depending on the product and project requirements.

Can I request a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Sample development can be arranged so you can review the design, fit, materials, and workmanship before confirming production. Any applicable sample charges are discussed in advance.

Can I provide my own fabric and materials?

Yes. You can supply your own fabrics and accessories, provided they are suitable for the requested manufacturing process.

Can HOUSE OF THREAD source the fabrics for my order?

Yes. We can discuss sourcing suitable fabrics and accessories according to your design, budget, required quality, and material availability.

Production & quality

How long does production take?

Production timelines depend on the design, quantity, materials, and current production schedule. We confirm the estimated lead time after reviewing your project requirements.

Can you accommodate urgent or time-sensitive orders?

Urgent requests can be evaluated based on complexity, quantity, material availability, and production capacity. We discuss what is realistically achievable before confirming a delivery deadline.

How do you maintain product quality?

Our production approach focuses on accurate specifications, suitable materials, careful workmanship, and quality checks throughout the manufacturing process, with final inspection before delivery.

Quotations, delivery & terms

How do I receive a manufacturing quotation?

You can submit a request through our website, contact us on WhatsApp, or send an email. Share your product details, estimated quantity, fabric preferences, and design references so we can assess the project and prepare a quotation.

How is the manufacturing price calculated?

Pricing depends on the product design, materials, quantity, construction complexity, finishing requirements, and any additional services requested. Each quotation is prepared according to the project's specifications.

What are your payment terms?

Payment terms are discussed and confirmed as part of the quotation and order agreement, before production begins.

Do you accept international orders and arrange shipping?

Yes. We welcome inquiries from clients outside Lebanon. International shipping options, costs, documentation, and estimated transit times are reviewed according to the destination, order size, and applicable requirements.

Will my designs and project information be kept confidential?

We recognize the importance of protecting client designs and business information. Confidentiality requirements, including any requested non-disclosure agreement, can be discussed before sensitive project materials are shared.
