How we work

From Vision to Delivery.

  1. 01

    Tell Us Your Idea

    Send a ready design or discuss an unfinished idea, including product type and required quantity.

  2. 02

    Develop & Define

    Review fabric, patterns, specifications, sampling needs and cost drivers.

  3. 03

    Plan & Quote

    Confirm specifications, quoted price, production time and any sample or development costs.

  4. 04

    Approve the Sample

    Review and approve a sample when agreed before bulk manufacturing.

  5. 05

    Make & Inspect

    Manufacture to the agreed specifications and check workmanship and finishing.

  6. 06

    Arrange Delivery

    Coordinate collection or shipping options according to location, availability and terms.
