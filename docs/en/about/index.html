About Us

A Legacy of Craftsmanship. A Vision for What’s Next.

Rooted in sewing and manufacturing experience since 2014.

Our story

Before the first stitch.

At HOUSE OF THREAD, craftsmanship is more than a skill — it is the foundation of everything we create. Drawing on sewing and manufacturing experience dating back to 2014, we combine years of practical knowledge with a contemporary vision for quality, creativity, and precision.

From fashion collections and tailored uniforms to hospitality linens, healthcare bedding, and custom-sewn products, we work with brands, businesses, and institutions to transform ideas into thoughtfully crafted solutions.

We believe exceptional manufacturing begins long before the first stitch. It starts with understanding your vision, refining every detail, selecting the right materials, and developing a product that meets your expectations.

Whether you arrive with a complete design or simply an idea waiting to take shape, HOUSE OF THREAD is where creativity meets craftsmanship — and where every detail matters.
